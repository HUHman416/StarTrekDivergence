## Away teams and changing eras

- Continuous first-person movement and strafing, mouse/keyboard aiming, phaser fire, energy cells, and live enemy encounters.
- Autonomous security and unaffiliated raiders; walls provide cover. Stun, high power, field medicine, ceasefire, pause, and emergency recall support different approaches.
- Four calendar-driven command themes: early warp, Constitution, refit, and next generation. Start a new campaign in any era or progress naturally.
- Era-specific sidearms with distinct visuals and stats; later weapons require research as their dates arrive. Existing fleet research also benefits away teams.
- v0.1–v0.3 save migration, including safe migration of active away missions. Exact movement, damage, cells and enemies persist in v0.4 saves.

This is a **2.5D FPS prototype** using original procedural artwork. Six scenarios still share a floor plan. Sketchfab models were researched and shortlisted, but downloading requires sign-in; no third-party model is included. Later starts use the existing alternate-history founding chapter, not a new era-specific story.

Export your campaign from Academy & saves before updating. Native desktop builds and the mod manager remain planned milestones.
