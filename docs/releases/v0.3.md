## Star Trek Divergence v0.3 — The wider frontier

Made entirely with ChatGPT, with concepts and ideas by HUHman.

- **LCARS interface:** orange elbows, pastel control segments, dark displays, and responsive navigation inspired by HUHman's LCARS Command Interface.
- **18 destinations:** six research discoveries, six away-mission sites, and four one-time field projects give fleets new reasons to explore.
- **First contact:** diplomacy and civilization names are revealed by arriving at their home systems or meeting visitors in your territory. The opening Vulcan delegation is your first contact; further origin delegations arrive as the council chapter advances. You choose relationships afterward.
- **Ship comparisons:** all four designs and every owned ship appear in a comparison table with hull, shields, weapons, costs, roles, and current research bonuses. The starting light escort retains its original stats.
- **Away teams:** a first-person grid exploration prototype with movement, a scanner map, three interactive objectives per site, preservation/recovery choices, safe recall, and mid-mission saves. Six themed scenarios currently share a corridor layout.
- **Monthly events:** a 35% chance each month of a decision from five event types. Every event includes a choice requiring no stored resources.
- **Advance several months:** choose 1, 3, 6, or 12 months. Progress stops for decisions, first contact, fleet arrivals, delayed mission results, or new diplomatic aid requests. Combat and deployed away teams pause time.
- **Save continuity:** v0.1/v0.2 imports migrate to format 3 without losing ship identities, wars, treaties, or campaign history. Old known contacts stay known; new campaigns use discovery rules.

## Download options

- `StarTrekDivergence-v0.3.html` — the entire game in one file.
- `StarTrekDivergence-v0.3.zip` — that same file plus a quick-start guide.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` — download checksums.

Export your campaign from **Academy & saves** before replacing your old game, then import the JSON into v0.3.

This remains a browser alpha. Native Linux/Windows installers and a built-in mod manager are later milestones. No multiplayer or complete faction-specific Klingon/Romulan story is included. The original Alpha and Alpha-v0.2 releases remain available.
